How We Work

Standard Engineering Workflow

A disciplined 5-stage project execution framework ensuring safety, budget compliance, and structural integrity.

01

Site Survey & Geotechnical

Topographical mapping, soil bearing tests, structural auditing, and environmental impact assessment.

02

Structural Design & CAD

BIM 3D modeling, load calculation sheets, MEP drawings, and statutory regulatory approvals.

03

Procurement & Mobilization

Sourcing certified steel and high-grade concrete, heavy machinery mobilization, and site HSE setup.

04

Execution & Quality Control

Phase-by-phase construction with ongoing cube testing, slump testing, and client site reporting.

05

Commissioning & Handover

Final structural testing, MEP system commissioning, snag list clearance, and practical completion handover.
